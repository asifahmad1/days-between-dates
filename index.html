Formats recognised: 2026-01-05 (ISO), 05/01/2026 or 05-01-2026 (day/month/year), and 5 Jan 2026 / Jan 5, 2026. When a slash date could be read two ways (e.g. 05/06/2026), it is read as day/month/year — the convention in Pakistan and most of the world. Each line needs two dates; lines without a pair are reported, never guessed.

How it counts

The difference is the number of midnights between the two dates — the standard used by calendars and project tools (20 Oct → 21 Oct = 1 day). Business days count Mondays–Fridays inside the same span. The years/months/days breakdown is a true calendar breakdown (e.g. 31 Jan → 1 Mar in a leap year = 1 month, 1 day). Public holidays are country-specific and are not deducted.
